# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct (summary adoption)

CipherPost adopts the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/)
v2.1. In short: be respectful, be constructive, no harassment of any kind.

- **Our pledge:** a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of
  background or identity.
- **Our standards:** empathetic, respectful communication; constructive
  feedback focused on the work; no trolling, insults, or personal attacks.
- **Enforcement:** violations may lead to warnings, temporary bans, or
  permanent removal at maintainer discretion. Report privately to the
  maintainers (see `SECURITY.md` for the private contact process).
